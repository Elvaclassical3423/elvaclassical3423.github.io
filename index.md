## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### 🚫 agentry Won't Start

- **Check your internet connection** - agentry needs internet to work.
- **Make sure no other program is using the same port** - Try closing other applications.
- **Restart your computer** - Sometimes a fresh start fixes everything.

### 🔄 Agents Not Responding

- **Check your API key** - Make sure it's entered correctly in Settings.
- **Wait a moment** - Sometimes agents take a few seconds to "wake up."
- **Refresh the page** - Press F5 or Ctrl+R in your browser.

### 💾 Lost Your Data?

- agentry saves your agents and conversations automatically.
- If you close the program, just restart it and everything will be there.
